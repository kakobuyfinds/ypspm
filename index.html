Buying guides

FSBuy product links and sizing

Open the original listing to confirm the seller, selected colour, size and stock status. If a saved Weidian product link opens a shop instead of an item, look up the original listing ID before ordering. Check the variant again whenever you follow a replacement link.

For sneaker size conversion, compare foot length and the seller’s insole measurement in centimetres. For a hoodie, lay a similar garment flat and compare chest width, body length and sleeve length. Save the variant and the date you checked it alongside the link.

FSBuy QC photos

Quality control (QC) photos are warehouse inspection photos. Match the size label and colour to your order first. For shoes, check the left and right pair side by side, including the heel and outsole. For a jersey, ask for a close-up of the badge and any name or number you selected.

Make extra photo requests specific. Ask for a ruler across the chest, a close-up of the jacket zipper or a view of the bracelet clasp fully closed. Check the warehouse’s return window while you still have time to act.

FSBuy shipping estimate

Compare quotes using the same destination postcode, packed dimensions and parcel weight. A shoebox or bulky jacket can raise volumetric weight. Ask which weight the route bills against and compare packing options before selecting a courier.

Budget for the item, domestic delivery, any service charges, international freight and any import charges collected separately. For the US, UK, Canada or EU, enter the actual destination and check what the quote includes before paying.

Frequently asked questions

What is an FSBuy spreadsheet?

It is a directory or shortlist for organizing product links by category. This independent guide opens CAFANS categories and explains which details to check before saving a listing.

Can QC photos prove a branded item is authentic?

No. Warehouse QC photos can show visible condition, labels and measurements. For authenticity, use evidence of provenance and an authorized seller or a qualified authentication service.

Why can the shipping quote change after packing?

The carrier may bill by packed dimensions or scale weight. Confirm the billable weight, available route and included fees once the parcel is packed.

Where do the category links go?

The category links open CAFANS. FSBuy and the featured brands are referenced for shopping research and style illustration.
